01 — The Brief

A pay-per-call business that
lives inside GoHighLevel

BlueUmbrella sells exclusive inbound debt-settlement calls. Debtors call in from ad campaigns, get pre-screened, and qualified calls transfer live to debt-settlement companies who pay per call.

The ask: a public-facing platform to sign those companies as clients — a premium landing page whose entire job is to get an operator to book an intro call, with every booking flowing straight into the client's GoHighLevel CRM. Not a contact form. A booking engine.

03 — The Hard Part

Booking a real GoHighLevel slot
from a custom front end

The technically demanding piece was making a bespoke, on-brand booking widget actually transact against GoHighLevel — instead of dropping in GoHighLevel's generic calendar embed. A private API token can't live in the browser, and the calendar's real availability has to drive what a visitor can pick.

The solution: a Cloudflare Worker sits between the site and GoHighLevel. The picker calls the Worker for live free-slots so only open times render; on submit, the Worker upserts the contact and books a confirmed appointment through the GoHighLevel API — resolving the visitor's timezone to the exact slot (DST-safe) and re-validating availability to prevent double-bookings.

Technical decision

Keeping the GoHighLevel token server-side in the Worker (never in the browser) let the custom UI stay pixel-perfect while booking real appointments securely. CORS is locked to the site's own origins, and the chosen slot's exact ISO time is sent through — so what the visitor sees is exactly what lands on the calendar.
