About Me

Precision meets creativity

I'm Roi Steven Gozun. My background covers business operations, financial underwriting, and client management, with a Civil Engineering degree behind it. Two fields that don't tolerate sloppy work, which is how I learned to catch things before they cost something.

Now I apply that same precision to frontend development and web design. I also handle graphic design and video editing, so most digital projects I take on stay with one pair of hands from brief to delivery.
